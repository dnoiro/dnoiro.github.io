About / Background

Hi, I’m Daniel.

An electrical engineer in South Carolina, interested in how hardware and software work together.

I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Clemson University in May 2025. I now work as an Energy Management System Engineer at Santee Cooper.

My professional work involves real-time system information, telemetry, engineering displays, and automation. Outside that work, I’m developing my foundation in digital design, embedded programming, and FPGA implementation.

I’m especially interested in roles where I can work close to the hardware: understanding a signal, implementing the logic, and connecting software behavior to a physical system.
