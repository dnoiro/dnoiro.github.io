About

I’m an electrical engineer based in South Carolina, with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Clemson University. I work on energy management systems at Santee Cooper and am interested in PCB design and FPGA/RTL roles.

My hardware background includes schematic and multilayer PCB layout in Altium, embedded interfaces, and microcontroller programming. I’m building on my digital-design coursework through Verilog and Vivado labs on the PYNQ-Z2.
