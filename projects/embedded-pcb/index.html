Embedded hardware PCB design

Aeronix Technologies Group / Clemson University · Senior Design II · January–May 2025

As a laboratory assistant on an Aeronix-sponsored senior design project at Clemson, I created schematics and multilayer PCB layouts in Altium Designer. My work covered assigned embedded hardware circuits for sensing, programming/debugging, and expansion.

My contributions

CircuitSchematic and layout work
IMU interfaceIntegrated an LSM6DSOX 6-axis IMU over SPI, including pin assignments, interrupt routing, and schematic and PCB layout.
Programming and debugDesigned a Micro-USB Type-B interface with ESD protection and board-level signal routing.
Expansion headerImplemented a 44-pin header exposing GPIO, SPI, I2C, and UART for external hardware connections.

I also produced hardware documentation and supported design reviews for these PCB subsystems.

Tools and interfaces

Altium Designer for schematic capture and PCB layout; SPI, I2C, UART, and GPIO interfaces.

I’ve continued developing these skills through Altium PCB design and simulation training. My MixedSim course exercise documents a component-tolerance study with the circuit and simulation plots.
