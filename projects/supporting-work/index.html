Space Flight Design: Hellbenders I

Clemson Creative Inquiry · Team project · 2022

Our team designed a rocket payload to measure temperature, pressure, humidity, altitude, and GPS location during flight, with the goal of studying atmospheric conditions and rocket performance.

Payload design

The review proposed a Teensy 4.1 flight computer, a SparkFun environmental combo sensor connected over I2C, a GPS module connected over UART, and SD-card data logging. A battery and DC/DC converter supplied power, with the electronics housed in a 3D-printed payload enclosure.

Prototyping & test plan

The plan called for breadboard checks before soldering the payload onto perfboard. Testing covered power and connection checks before mating hardware, GPS lock, flight-sequence operation, payload durability, and verification that saved SD-card data could be read back.

These details describe the design and planned tests documented at the Critical Design Review. The video below shows the team’s launch.
